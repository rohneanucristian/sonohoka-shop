The Ritual

How to get the most out of one bottle.
01

Shake

Give the bottle a good shake before every use to bring the mineral salts back into suspension.

02

Mist

Hold 20cm from towel-dried or dry lengths and mist evenly, root to end.

03

Scrunch

Scrunch through with your palms, or diffuse on low heat, for texture that lasts.
